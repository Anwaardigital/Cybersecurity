🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What We Collect

CyberSec Hub is a static educational website. We do not require accounts, and we do not collect names, emails, or personal details through the site itself (the newsletter form is a demo and submits nothing).

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which guides are helpful. Analytics collects anonymized usage data (pages visited, device type, approximate location). You can opt out with browser extensions or Do-Not-Track settings.

Cookies

Google Analytics may set cookies to distinguish visits. No advertising cookies are set by us directly.

External Links

Our guides link to official tool websites (e.g., kali.org, wireshark.org). Their privacy practices are their own.

Children

This site is educational and family-friendly, but it is written for teens and adults. Parents should supervise younger readers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
