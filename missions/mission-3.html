🔐 Mission 3: Password Cracker

MEDIUM  400 pts  ·  Simulate an attack on weak passwords — and learn what actually stays strong.

⚠️ Educational simulation with fake passwords. Never try to crack a real account — that is illegal. Only test passwords you own.

How to play

  1. For each password below, guess: does it crack fast or stay strong?
  2. Tap your guess on all 4 passwords, then run the simulation.
  3. Watch the simulated attacker work — then see if your guesses were right.
  4. All 4 correct = flag captured.
