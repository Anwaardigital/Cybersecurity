EASY  200 pts  ·  Decode hidden messages step by step and reveal the flag.

How to play

  1. Stage 1: Decode the Base64 message. Use the decoder tool below if you need it.
  2. Stage 2: The decoded word tells you the Caesar shift. Shift the secret text back to read the flag.
  3. Type the flag to complete the mission.
