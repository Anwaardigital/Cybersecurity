Ethical Hacking Career

From fundamentals to job-ready. The professional, legal route to becoming a penetration tester or security analyst.

Read this first — the ethical rule: Everything in this path is for defensive and authorized use only. Practice in your own lab or on systems you own, or with written permission from the owner. Testing anyone else's systems without permission is illegal in most countries — this site teaches the legal professional path only.

Path 3

Home User Security

Not studying for a career — just want your phone, WiFi, and family safe? Work through this action list.

This is a "do this, not just read" checklist. Read each guide, then complete the action before moving to the next step.

1

Password Security: The Complete Guide (2026)

Do this: Install a password manager and replace every reused password with a unique passphrase — start with your email and bank.

2

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): The Complete Guide (2026)

Do this: Turn on 2FA today for your email, bank, and social accounts using an authenticator app — and save the backup codes.

3

Phishing Protection: How to Spot Fake Emails, SMS & Calls

Do this: Memorize the red flags and run the 10-second check on every suspicious email, SMS, or call before clicking or answering.

4

Mobile Security Guide: Lock Down Your Phone (2026)

Do this: Update your phone, set a strong screen lock, review app permissions, and turn on automatic backups.

5

WiFi Security Guide: Secure Your Home Network (2026)

Do this: Log into your router, change the default password, enable WPA3, and set up a guest network for visitors.

6

Ransomware Protection Guide: Keep Your Files Safe (2026)

Do this: Set up automatic backups of your photos and documents — a backup is the only true defense against ransomware.

7

10 Types of Cyber Attacks Explained (With Real Examples)

Do this: Read it once so you recognize common attacks — malware, scams, and fake calls — when they reach you.

8

Social Engineering Awareness: Don't Get Manipulated

Do this: Share these lessons with your family — elderly parents and kids are the most common targets of manipulation.

9

Password Security Testing: Audit Your Own Passwords

Do this: Audit the strength of your own passwords with the guide's methods and replace any weak ones you find.

10

Password Cracking Defense: How Attackers Guess Passwords (And How to Stop Them)

Do this: Learn how attackers guess passwords — brute force, dictionary attacks — so your defenses actually work. Defensive guide — no attack instructions
