🔄 Base64 Encoder / Decoder

Encode text to Base64 or decode Base64 back to text. Runs fully in your browser.

⚠️ Ethical Use Only: These tools are for defensive purposes — securing your own accounts and systems. Never use security knowledge against systems you do not own or lack written permission to test.
💡 Note: Base64 is encoding, not encryption — anyone can decode it. Never use Base64 to "hide" passwords or secrets.
