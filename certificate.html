Certificate of Completion

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Finish every guide in a learning path and earn a printable certificate with your name on it. Your name is stored only in your own browser — nothing is sent to any server.

Please read: this certificate confirms you completed a self-paced learning path on CyberSec Hub. It is not a government-recognized qualification and not a professional certification. Do not present it as a formal credential.

How it works: mark each guide as complete with the "Mark as complete" button at the bottom of the guide. When every guide in your chosen path is complete, the certificate appears here. Your progress is saved in your browser only — see our Privacy Policy.
